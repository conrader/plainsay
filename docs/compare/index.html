Plainsay is free. Premium is optional.

Plainsay Local is a complete, free, MIT-licensed Mac dictation app. No account, subscription, or weekly usage quota is required. Choose Whisper or Parakeet and keep speech recognition on your Mac. Other apps also have free options, which we describe below.

Optional Plainsay Cloud costs US$4/month or US$40/year, VAT included, and includes hosted transcription and Polishing, plus translation and email formatting. You do not need it for local dictation. Broader AI edits require a configured provider; a Cloud subscription alone does not enable Voice Edit.

How to choose a dictation app

  1. Use a real task. Dictate a message into an app you use every day. Include a name, a number, and a phrase in each language you speak.
  2. Check the whole data path. Local speech recognition and local text editing are separate choices. Hosted Polishing can send transcript text even when speech stays on-device.
  3. Compare ongoing costs. Look at free allowances, billing periods, and any provider charges. A paid feature list only matters if you use it.
  4. Check the workflow. Imported recordings, mobile writing, and everyday Mac dictation are different tasks. Read the individual comparisons before switching.
