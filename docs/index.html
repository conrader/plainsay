Inside Plainsay

Open source, transparent, safe

Trust isn't a claim here — it's something you can read. The Plainsay Mac client source is public under MIT. Every version already released under MIT remains free to fork, modify, and build yourself; third-party components and models have their own licenses.

WHAT'S OPEN

The Plainsay Mac client source includes its transcription and Polishing integrations, HUD, Settings, and Setup Assistant. Third-party components and models retain their own licenses. The Mac client sends no analytics or telemetry. Signed-update checks contact api.plainsay.app by default and can be disabled in Settings. Optional Cloud records the account, subscription, and usage metadata needed to operate the paid service.

WHAT ISN'T

The backend for the paid Cloud tier — it manages subscription state through Stripe, holds service-provider credentials, and records the account, subscription, and usage metadata needed for the paid service. The Plainsay Mac client source is open; the optional Cloud service is not.

Spoken in, written out

YOU SAID

um so basically can we uh move the standup to like ten thirty tomorrow because i have a a dentist thing in the morning

PLAINSAY TYPED

Can we move the standup to 10:30 tomorrow? I have a dentist appointment in the morning.

The cleanup pass removes fillers and false starts and fixes punctuation. Polishing is instructed to preserve wording, meaning, tone, and language; review important text. The provider is swappable (Gemini, OpenRouter, OpenAI, Claude, or a custom endpoint), it can be switched off entirely, and any failure falls back to the raw transcript instead of failing the dictation.

Why Plainsay

Reliability

Clear recovery paths after a recording finishes.

A configured Polishing provider fails

Falls back to the raw transcript automatically, with a HUD notice.

Nothing was focused to paste into

Text stays on the clipboard, with a HUD prompt to press ⌘V.

The app closes after recording finishes

Plainsay attempts to stage the finished audio before transcription. If staging succeeds, it can retry on the next launch.

A paste fails for any other reason

Plainsay adds the completed transcript to local history before attempting insertion.

Three ways to run it
