Plainsay vs superwhisper

Open Local mode, or a broader Pro app?

superwhisper is a capable proprietary app for macOS, Windows, and iOS. It offers local and cloud transcription, and its Pro tier can also run the optional language-model pass locally on macOS. Plainsay's MIT-licensed Mac client is narrower: Local is free and unmetered, while optional Cloud and Polishing choices are explicit.

Open, so you don't have to take our word for it

A closed-source app asks you to trust that it does what it says. An open one lets you read the code.

Plainsay's Mac client is MIT licensed and public — the pipeline, recorder, settings, and release tooling. You can inspect it, fork it, and build your own copy; the optional Plainsay Cloud backend is separate and not open source. superwhisper is proprietary: its terms say intellectual-property rights belong to the company or its licensors and prohibit extracting the app's source code.

Both can keep both stages local

Both apps separate speech recognition from an optional language-model pass, and both let you avoid that second pass.

Plainsay Polishing is switchable — use Plainsay Cloud, bring your own API key, point a compatible custom endpoint at a local model, or turn it off and keep the raw transcript. superwhisper also documents independent local or cloud choices for transcription and post-processing, including local language models on macOS. Its cloud requests pass through the superwhisper proxy; its official data-flow guide says API providers process them under zero-data-retention terms. Read superwhisper's data-flow guide.

Where superwhisper is the better pick — honestly

  • You need Windows or iOS — Plainsay is macOS-only today, superwhisper covers all three.
  • You want built-in meeting summaries, speaker separation, and audio/video file transcription out of the box. Meeting mode · File transcription
  • You want the broader predefined output-mode set — Message, Email, and Voice — without configuring your own cleanup prompt. Plainsay unlocks automatic email layout and Auto-translate for Cloud subscribers, and since v0.2.33 they work on the built-in Cloud route too, but that is still two styles rather than superwhisper's full mode catalog. Official modes guide

If instead you want a free, open-source Local mode with no account and a Polishing step you fully control — that's Plainsay.
