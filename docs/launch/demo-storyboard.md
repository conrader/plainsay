# Authentic 20–30 second demo storyboard

## Purpose

Prove the complete interaction in the shipping app: Local mode is selected,
the user holds a key, speaks in Polish and English, releases, and the actual
transcript lands at the cursor.

This is a product recording, not a concept mockup. Do not replace the generated
text, animate a fake cursor, speed up transcription, or splice a successful
result onto a different dictation. A continuous take is preferred. If a pause
is trimmed, label the cut and retain the original recording.

## Before recording

- Install the exact notarized build that will be public at launch.
- Use a clean macOS account with no personal files, account names, email,
  notifications, clipboard history, or menu-bar secrets visible.
- Use macOS 14 or newer on Apple silicon and record the exact hardware, macOS,
  app version, engine, and model in the video description.
- Select **On this Mac** and confirm that the chosen Whisper or Parakeet model
  is ready. Download the model before the take; do not present this as a
  first-launch recording.
- Turn Polishing off for the main take so the recording proves local speech
  recognition without a hosted text-processing path.
- Open a new, empty document in a standard app such as Notes.
- Use the default Right Command shortcut and make the HUD visible.
- Record at native resolution with a large enough cursor and text size to read
  on a phone. Capture the whole relevant app window, not only the final text.
- Do one rehearsal only to check framing and audio level. The published take
  must show a real run from speech to insertion.

## Spoken prompts

Use these as prompts, not as promised output:

- Polish: “Jutro o dziewiątej wyślijmy klientowi poprawioną wersję raportu.”
- English: “After lunch, move the release checklist into the project notes.”

Read naturally. Keep the transcript Plainsay actually produces. If a take is
unusable, record the whole take again rather than correcting the document by
hand. Save the original screen recording alongside the edited export.

## Timeline

| Time | Picture | Audio/action | Proof delivered |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Plainsay Speech settings, with **On this Mac** and the ready local engine visible. | No narration. | Local is a real, selected mode. |
| 3–5 s | Switch to the empty Notes document. | Natural room sound or silence. | The destination begins empty. |
| 5–12 s | HUD appears while Right Command is held. The first actual transcript lands after release. | Speak the Polish prompt. | Polish dictation and cursor insertion. |
| 12–19 s | On a new line, show the same HUD and actual insertion again. | Speak the English prompt. | Language switching in the same real workflow. |
| 19–25 s | Freeze on the untouched output, then use a simple end card. | Optional quiet key-release sound; no voice-over needed. | Clear product and requirements. |

End-card copy:

> Hold a key. Speak. Release.<br>
> Free Local mode · MIT-licensed Mac client<br>
> macOS 14+ · Apple silicon · plainsay.app

Small but readable disclosure:

> 12 MB client DMG; local models download separately (about 475–632 MB).

## Export set

- Master: 1440p or higher, 30 fps, H.264, with the actual cursor and HUD.
- Repository/website: 16:9 MP4, 20–30 seconds, under a practical page-load
  size; provide a poster frame and captions/transcript.
- Social crop: 1:1 or 4:5 derived from the same take. Do not recreate the
  dictation for the crop.
- GIF only if a platform cannot play MP4. Keep the MP4 as the source of truth;
  GIF compression must not make the inserted text unreadable.

## Review checklist

- The build in the recording is the downloadable build.
- The first frame visibly says **On this Mac** or **Local**.
- No Cloud request or hosted Polishing is involved in the demonstrated take.
- Both dictated passages and both resulting transcripts are legible.
- There is no manual correction between release and the final frame.
- No personal or secret information appears in a frame, notification, menu,
  URL, clipboard, or window title.
- The caption says that the client is about 12 MB and models are separate.
- The caption states macOS 14+ and Apple silicon.
- The description links to the repository, DMG, benchmark, and privacy table.
