# Attribution

The audio in `audio/` and the reference transcripts embedded in
`librispeech-test-clean-manifest.json` are a 50-utterance subset of the
`test-clean` portion of the **LibriSpeech ASR corpus**:

> V. Panayotov, G. Chen, D. Povey and S. Khudanpur, "Librispeech: An ASR
> corpus based on public domain audio books," 2015 IEEE International
> Conference on Acoustics, Speech and Signal Processing (ICASSP), 2015,
> pp. 5206-5210.

Source: <https://www.openslr.org/12/>
License: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

**Changes made**: this is a subset (50 of 2,620 `test-clean` utterances,
selected as described in [`../../BENCHMARK.md`](../../BENCHMARK.md)) with no
other modification to the audio or transcripts.
